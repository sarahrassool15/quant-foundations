# quant-foundations

I'm doing a bunch of projects alongside my classes to test out some _fairly ball_ experiments. Just want to see how what I’m learning applies to the real world and pick up some industry skills.
These include short experiments (and other ideas I've entertained) from my self-study of the math behind quant finance. I hope to post them all here as a timeline of where my thoughts and curiosity went. I'll post all the results, code, etc. here. Just decided now to push them to the git because why not. 
| | Project | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Dice, Fourier and the shape of a sum](01-dice-fourier) | What's the exact distribution of 50 dice, how fast can I get it, and how wrong is the bell curve? |
| 02 | [The leakage hunter](02-leakage-hunter) | How much predictive power can a pipeline "find" in pure noise? |
| 03 | [Does Bitcoin's maximum behave like Brownian motion's?](03-bitcoin-maximum) | How far does Bitcoin wander from the open each day, and is that what Brownian motion predicts? |
| 04 | [Kelly betting, and why traders bet half](04-kelly) | How much should you risk when your wealth multiplies, and what changes when you don't know your edge? |
| 05 | [From Monopoly to market regimes](05-markov-regimes) | Does Bitcoin's volatility behave like a Markov chain? |
| 06 | [Gambler's ruin on a leveraged long](06-gamblers-ruin) | With a 10x Bitcoin long, which comes first, liquidation or take profit? |
| 07 | [Random walkers and the heat equation](07-walkers-heat) | Is the heat equation really just a crowd of people flipping coins? |
